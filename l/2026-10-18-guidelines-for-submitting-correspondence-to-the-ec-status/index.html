The letter

18 October 2026
To: The Executive Committee, Greenway Terraces, UP12457, c/o Grady Strata ()
From: Steven, Unit 151, 1 Rowland Rees Crescent, Greenway ACT
Re: Guidelines for Submitting Correspondence to the EC – status, statutory rights, item 8 (AI) and unanswered correspondence
1. Status of the Guidelines and the law
• The Guidelines call themselves “advice to owners”. Please confirm they are not rules of the owners corporation and identify any resolution adopting them.
• I can find nothing in the Unit Titles (Management) Act 2011 (ACT) prescribing the length, format, channel or authorship of owner correspondence.
• Statutory rights are unaffected: records must be made available within 14 days of a written request (s 120A); executive members’ names and addresses within 14 days, free (s 117); a general meeting within 28 days of a written request (Schedule 3). Items 6 (portal first), 7 (time frames) and 9 (return of multi-page letters) cannot reduce or delay these. Please confirm requests under the Act will be accepted, with time running from receipt, whatever their length or format.
2. Item 8 (AI) is unjustified and unworkable
• The author answers for a letter’s content whatever tools were used, and the EC must test every statement on its merits anyway, whether written by hand, a lawyer, a friend or software.
• Singling out one tool implies content may be discounted for how it was drafted, not what it says. “AI content” is undefined (spell-check? grammar? translation?) and no consequence is stated. Nothing in the Act requires it. Please withdraw it.
3. What the record shows
• Seven members were elected at the 26 August 2026 AGM to serve owners. Reading and answering a short question is not a specialist skill.
• Between 13 December 2025 and 17 August 2026 I sent about 45 items to Grady Strata and the Committee. My register (31 August 2026) lists 34 as unanswered or answered only in general terms. The last written reply to me was dated 21 March 2026.
• Video cameras: post-implementation review asked 22 March and again 7 May (has trespass actually fallen?). No reply.
• Pool-area palms: eight questions on 9 April on what happened to them and why they were replaced, chased 22 April and 26 May (5 June deadline). No reply.
• Playground softfall: asked 30 March whether it still met the fall-safety standard; the works went ahead from June without an answer.
• Town hall minutes and authority for the playground works: asked 9 April and 26 May. Pool safety protocol: raised five times, 7 February to 26 May. Formal complaint: 20 March and 7 April, never acknowledged.
4. What is the Committee trying to achieve?
• Single-subject questions such as these are not “large volumes of extremely detailed correspondence”. If the aim is workload, items 1–5 serve it; items 8 and 9 serve only to provide grounds to return or discount letters.
• Please state in writing the problem the Guidelines address, and the authority for items 8 and 9.
I ask for a written reply by Friday 30 October 2026 covering: withdrawal of item 8; the confirmations in section 1; each register item answered, closed with reasons, or given a date; and who is responsible for answering owner correspondence.
Yours sincerely,
Steven, Unit 151, Greenway Terraces
