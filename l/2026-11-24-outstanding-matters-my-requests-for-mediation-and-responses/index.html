Outstanding matters, my requests for mediation and responses, and my intended application to ACAT

Dear Committee members,

I write to record that I have sought mediation and responses on a number of matters, that they remain unaddressed, and that I intend, in the new year, to lodge an application with the ACT Civil and Administrative Tribunal (ACAT) if they are not addressed. I do not want to take that step. I am taking it because the committee has not engaged with the concerns I have raised or responded to them.

1. What I have asked for
• I asked for independent mediation about the governance, administration and management of Greenway Terraces.
• 30 October 2026: I followed up, noting that I had received no formal response, and asked for the committee’s collective written position within 14 calendar days. That period has passed.
• 14 October 2026 and 26 October 2026: I asked that owners be able to take part by video link. I also asked that written suggestions be reported to the Town Hall and that a summary be sent to owners and residents afterwards.
• 24 November 2026: I wrote about the 19 November meeting, the motions, votes and notices behind the decisions, the proposed audio and video recording, and electronic voting, and asked for a reply by 8 December 2026.
I have no record of a reply to any of these.

2. Summary of the application I intend to lodge
The application would be a dispute between an owner and the executive committee under s 125 of the Unit Titles (Management) Act 2011. I would ask ACAT, under s 129, to order:

1. access to the records behind the 19 November 2026 decisions, including the notice and agenda, the motions, the mover, seconder and each member’s vote, the quorum record, the Town Hall notice and attendance, the security quotes, and any legal or privacy advice;
2. a general meeting, with motions to allow owners to take part by phone or video link, to adopt pre-meeting electronic voting, and to put the security measures to owners with costs and funding source;
3. that no audio-recording capability be installed or activated, and no related contracts signed, until owners have decided those motions;
4. in the alternative, the repeal or amendment of the resolutions at items 2.1.2, 2.1.4 and 2.1.6 so that they take effect only after owners approve them; and
5. any other order ACAT considers reasonably necessary.
The grounds are that the executive committee acts as the owners corporation directs by resolution at a general meeting (s 35); that the only direction cited, item 14 of the Improvement Plan, is to investigate options; that the minutes do not record the votes, notices or quorum; and that owners cannot take part remotely although the Act allows it.

3. Outstanding matters
• Correspondence: how owners’ letters reach the committee, who answers them, and a process for following up unanswered matters.
• Minutes and decision records: clear records of decisions, expenditure approvals, responsibilities and actions.
• Subcommittees (Energy, Security, Building Defects, Communications): the establishing decisions, membership, terms of reference, any delegated spending authority and reporting.
• Financial transparency: how expenditure, project costs and sinking fund planning are approved and reported.
• Security: CCTV effectiveness, access controls, the approved guard, cameras with two-way audio, the duress alarm and door locks, with costs, funding and lawfulness.
• Community battery: the method accepted, who owns, insures and maintains it, and whether owners must approve it.
• Consultation and participation: the form of the 14 November Town Hall, the authority of its recommendations, and how owners can take part, including remotely.
4. What I ask
Please provide the committee’s collective written position by 8 December 2026, signed by an authorised member, on whether it accepts mediation and how each outstanding matter will be addressed, with responsibility and timing. Please table this letter at the committee meeting on 10 December 2026. Please confirm in writing that it has been given to every executive member and the date it was given.

If the matters are not addressed, I intend to lodge the application in the new year.

Yours sincerely,

Stephen Prothero
Owner, Unit 151, Greenway Terraces (UP12457)
