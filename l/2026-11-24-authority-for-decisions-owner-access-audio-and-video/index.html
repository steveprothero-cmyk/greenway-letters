The letter

24 November 2026

Lulu Issa, Strata Manager
Grady Strata and Facilities
G2 / 65 Canberra Avenue, Griffith ACT

FOR ATTENTION BY EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE

Greenway Terraces – The Owners – Units Plan 12457
Executive Committee meeting of 19 November 2026: authority for decisions, owner access, audio and video recording, and electronic voting

Dear Lulu,

I am the owner of Unit 151. In this letter “the Act” means the Unit Titles (Management) Act 2011. I write about the Executive Committee (EC) meeting held in the Pool Room on 19 November 2026 and the minutes issued for it. Please provide this letter to every executive member and table it at the EC meeting on 10 December 2026.

1. Status of the meeting and owner access
Under the Act, decisions of owners are made at a general meeting, to which Schedule 3 applies (s 17). The executive committee exercises the functions of the owners corporation (s 35(1)). It must do so either as the corporation directs by resolution at a general meeting or, without such a resolution, as the committee considers appropriate (s 35(3)). A decision of the EC is therefore not a decision of owners. A Town Hall is not a general meeting unless it was called and run under Schedule 3, and its recommendations are not a direction from owners.

The minutes record an EC meeting attended by five executive members, with the manager’s representatives not in attendance. I ask:

(a) Was any part of that meeting a general meeting of the owners corporation? If so, please provide the notice and the record of owners present and votes. If not, please confirm that no resolution of the owners corporation was passed.

(b) What form of meeting was the Town Hall held on 14 November 2026: a general meeting under Schedule 3, an EC meeting, or an informal consultation? What authority do you say its recommendations carry? The minutes treat them as the basis for six approved motions.

(c) Were owners and residents told of the 19 November meeting, and could they attend, observe or listen? If not, why not? Why was no remote link offered, given that the Act allows executive committee meetings to be held by communication link (Sch 2, s 2.8) and general meetings likewise (Sch 3, s 3.1)?

(d) Who acted as secretary and took the minutes, and on what date were they issued to executive members?

2. My email of 26 October 2026
On 26 October 2026 I wrote to you for the Committee about the Town Hall. Section 4 of that email read: “The meeting is at 5:00pm on a Saturday, so I ask that a video link be offered, that written suggestions received by email be reported to the meeting, and that a short summary of what was said be sent to owners and residents afterwards.” I have no record of a reply, or of a link being provided. Please tell me:

(a) why that request was not answered;

(b) whether the email was given to the Committee and, if so, when;

(c) whether a video link was offered for the Town Hall and, if not, why not;

(d) whether written suggestions received by email, including mine, were reported to the meeting and, if not, why not; and

(e) whether a summary of what was said at the Town Hall was sent to owners and residents and, if not, why not and when it will be.

3. What was approved, and on what authority
Item 2.2.2 quotes item 14 of the AGM-approved Improvement Plan: “Investigate options to improve the pool recreation area”. A resolution to investigate options is not approval to replace a fence, install cameras, locks or alarms, engage a guard, or spend money. Item 14 also does not cover the security measures in items 2.1.1 to 2.1.6. Item 2.2.3 accepts that a general meeting is needed for the fence, yet items 2.1.2, 2.1.4 and 2.1.6 approve installation without one. I ask for:

(a) the exact wording of each motion approved at items 2.1.1 to 2.1.6, 2.4 (the Leader Security guard service) and 2.5 (the community battery), with the mover, the seconder and how each executive member voted (for, against or abstain);

(b) confirmation, with the supporting records, that the meeting was called and run in accordance with the Act and could approve each matter: written notice of at least 7 days stating the business was given to every executive member (Sch 2, s 2.8(2)); a quorum was present when each matter was decided (s 2.9); each matter was decided by a majority of members present and voting (s 2.10); and any conflicts of interest were declared;

(c) the authority and funding source for each approved expense, noting that the 2026 AGM package costed a guard service at about $110,000 and described it as impractical; and

(d) confirmation of whether any contract, including with Leader Security, has been signed or any work ordered, and if any matter was beyond the EC’s power, that it will go to a general meeting before anything is committed.

4. Proposed audio and video recording
Items 2.1.1, 2.1.2 and 2.1.6 approve monitoring of the camera network, new cameras “with 2 way audio capability” in front of and behind the Pool Room, and electronic access control. The minutes say nothing about privacy or lawfulness. My understanding is that the Listening Devices Act 1992 (ACT) restricts the use of a listening device to record a private conversation, and that a camera with a microphone may be such a device. I also understand the Workplace Privacy Act 2011 (ACT) requires notice where workers, such as a guard or contractors, are subject to surveillance. Please advise:

(a) Has legal advice been obtained on the lawfulness of the proposal? If so, please provide it. If not, please say so.

(b) Will the new cameras record sound, or only allow live two-way talk? Where would any sound be recorded, monitored and retained?

(c) The Pool Room area is where residents, guests and children gather and talk. How will their privacy be protected?

(d) What signage, retention period, access controls and access log will apply to footage, who may view it, and how may residents request it?

(e) Does the EC consider it has authority to approve this without owners deciding at a general meeting? No costs or funding source are recorded for any of these items.

Until these questions are answered, I ask that no audio-recording capability be installed or activated.

5. Electronic voting and remote attendance – urgent
I asked for a video link on 14 October 2026 and again on 26 October 2026 for the Town Hall. I have had no reply to either request. Requiring owners to attend on site excludes owners who are unwell, elderly, working, caring for others, interstate or overseas. The Act already provides the tools to fix this:

(i) Sch 3, s 3.1(2) and (3): the owners corporation may authorise a general meeting to be held by phone or internet link, and a person taking part is taken to be present.

(ii) Sch 3, s 3.31A: the owners corporation may, by resolution passed at a general meeting, agree to a way of voting on a matter or class of matters. The Unit Titles (Management) Regulation permits pre-meeting voting by email or other electronic means for matters other than elections.

The minutes record that a general meeting will be needed on the pool fence. I ask that:

(1) the EC resolve on 10 December 2026 to call that general meeting at the earliest practicable date;

(2) the notice include a motion under Sch 3, s 3.31A adopting pre-meeting electronic voting;

(3) the meeting be held with a communication link so owners can attend and vote remotely; and

(4) Grady confirm in writing whether it can administer electronic voting and remote attendance, and at what cost.

6. Records
Under s 120A of the Act, I request inspection and copies of: the notice and agenda issued to executive members for the 19 November meeting; the notice and attendance record for the 14 November Town Hall and any record of its recommendations; the Leader Security quote and any other quotes obtained for security services; and any legal advice or privacy assessment on the CCTV and audio proposal.

I ask for a written reply by 8 December 2026.

Yours sincerely,

Stephen Prothero
