Dear Committee Members

Maintenance Plan for Greenway Terraces (QIA job 179351): inconsistencies and out-of-date content — response requested by Friday 30 October 2026

1. Purpose
I have reviewed the Owners Corporation’s ACT Maintenance Plan spreadsheet for Greenway Terraces (Plan No 12457, Class A), dated 15 August 2022 and prepared with the 2022 QIA sinking fund (job 179350). I compared it with its own contents, with the 2022 sinking fund, and with the current sinking fund (version 4, 4 August 2025).

The plan contains internal inconsistencies, shows no inspection or completion records for most of its period, and has not been updated to reflect the 2025 sinking fund or assets added since 2022. I ask the Committee to respond in writing to the questions in section 4.

2. What I found
Scheduling does not follow the stated asset lives. Of the 26 items with more than one scheduled event, 21 are spaced differently from their own “estimated overall life”. Examples: irrigation (row 68) has a 20-year life but is scheduled every 5 years; common-area lighting (row 58) has a 15-year life but is scheduled every 3 years; door hardware (row 61) has a 20-year life but is scheduled every 3 years; playground and gym equipment (rows 104 and 105) have an 8-year life but are scheduled every 6 years; hand dryers (row 77) have a 6-year life but are scheduled 8 years apart. The marks appear to follow the sinking fund’s maintenance and accrual cycles rather than replacement events, and the plan does not say so.

Pool items are internally contradictory. The cartridge filter (row 97) has a 15-year life but is marked in every year from 2022 to 2036; the 2022 sinking fund prices it as an annual item. The pool surface (row 94) has a 40-year life but is marked in five consecutive years (2032–2036); the sinking fund treats those as partial accruals toward a refurbishment at 15 years (16 years in version 4).

Install dates are blank and the lives cannot be reconciled. The Install Date column is empty throughout. Working back from the first scheduled event and the stated life gives implied installation years from 1978 to 2023, which cannot be correct for a scheme whose units plan appears to date from 2020. Fire hose reels imply 2010 and hydrant valve assemblies imply 2000. Garage doors have a 30-year life but are marked in 2036.

Other mismatches. Toilet and basin replacement (rows 80 and 81) is marked 2036; the 2022 sinking fund first applies it in 2039. Four items have an “Ad Hoc” life but also carry a scheduled date (rows 39, 66, 67 and 75). I have been unable to identify a matching line in the 2022 sinking fund for balcony membrane (row 22), baluster fencing (row 51), planters (row 67) or air-conditioner units (row 106). Several rows duplicate or overlap each other (rows 78 and 107; 68 and 69; 104 and 105; 74, 80 and 81; 92, 93 and 97).

Inspection records are missing and fire items appear overdue. Only three rows record a date last inspected: fire hose reels and portable extinguishers (1 August 2021) and hydrant valve assemblies (1 February 2022). The plan lists all three at six-monthly intervals. No other row has an inspection date, and the plan contains no completion status for any item scheduled in 2022 to 2026.

Standards references. Emergency and exit lighting (row 57) cites AS2293.2-1995. The current edition is AS/NZS 2293.2:2019. The switchboard and RCD row (row 63) is listed as “Biannually”, which is ambiguous, and cites AS/NZS 3000:2018, which is the wiring rules; whether it is the appropriate reference for in-service inspection should be confirmed. No standard is cited for the playground, softfall, pool barrier or gates, although the Committee’s own softfall papers refer to AS 4422:2016 and AS 4685. Pool fence fixings and balustrade fixings are assigned to “OC” only.

The plan is out of date. It is headed “Initial Maintenance Plan, 15 August 2022” and carries no revision date. It contains no costs, totals or status fields. It does not list CCTV, the rainwater tank and pump, stormwater pit edges and grates, the playground and gym softfall surfaces, the pergola shade sails, the defibrillator, or the sewer backup generator proposed in the energy management plan. Version 4 of the sinking fund re-times several lines (for example garage doors and security gates every three years, and additional painting cycles in 2028, 2032 and 2036, plus switchboard works in 2025 and 2029), none of which is reflected in the plan.

3. Why this matters
The maintenance plan is the document that tells the Owners Corporation, its strata manager and its contractors what must be inspected, how often and by whom. Where it is inconsistent or out of date, it cannot reliably support the sinking fund, the budget or the scheduling of safety-related and compliance inspections, and it cannot show owners that those inspections have been done.

4. What I ask the Committee to answer
1. Is the August 2022 plan the maintenance plan currently adopted by the Owners Corporation? If a later version exists, please provide it.
2. Has the plan been reviewed or updated since 15 August 2022? If so, when, and by whom?
3. Please provide the inspection and service records for fire hose reels, portable fire extinguishers, hydrant valve assemblies, emergency and exit lighting, and the electrical switchboards and RCDs since 1 August 2021. If no such records exist for any item, please say so.
4. Please confirm, for each item scheduled in 2022 to 2026, whether the work was completed, and provide the records of completion or state that none exist.
5. Does the Committee accept the inconsistencies in section 2 (including the pool filter and pool surface entries, the blank install dates and the AS2293.2-1995 reference)? If any is disputed, please say which and why.
6. Will the plan be revised and reconciled with version 4 of the sinking fund? If so, by whom, and by what date?
7. Who is responsible for maintaining the plan and for recording inspections against it?
5. Timing
Please respond in writing by Friday 30 October 2026. If any document I have asked for does not exist, a statement to that effect is a complete answer. If the Committee needs longer for any item, please nominate a date and give the reason.

Yours sincerely

Stephen W Prothero
Owner, Unit 151
